# Electricians, Plumbers, Handymen and Locksmiths in Pattaya: SiamFind lead list

Research date: 2026-11-15. Scope: Pattaya, Jomtien, Naklua, Pratumnak, East Pattaya, Huay Yai, Bang Saray, Nong Prue. Excluded as already on SiamFind: ServiceThai24, Pattaya Realty Property Maintenance, Mots Services.

Overall caveat: Facebook pages, Google Business pages, the aseannow.com forum and pattayaprestigeproperties.com could not be fetched (login wall or HTTP 403). Many leads therefore come from the providers' own websites, Thai SEO landing pages and classified ads, so "active now" is often unconfirmed. Each lead has a confidence flag:
- **[A]**: own website shows 2025–2026 activity.
- **[B]**: a business contact is published, but recency is unclear or old.
- **[C]**: only a third-party mention, or the lead is old. Verify before outreach.

## Which providers exist and are active now (2025–2026 evidence), with contacts, area, languages and pitch note?

### Takeaway
I found about 25 candidate leads. Only a handful have strong 2025–2026 evidence: ACES Pattaya, Pattaya Home Improvement, and the Na Kluea locksmith site dated (c) 2569 / 2026. The Thai-market electricians, plumbers and locksmiths mostly advertise through keyword-stuffed Thai SEO sites, Google Sites, Blogspot and classifieds, with a phone number or LINE ID and no English. They have little web presence of their own, which makes them a good fit for free SiamFind pages, but each needs a phone or LINE check before onboarding. I did not reach the 8–12 per category target with verified-current leads for plumbers and locksmiths; the counts are below.

### Inferences
- Lead counts with at least a business contact: electricians about 3 strong (ACES, the 350 THB sole trader, Jomtien Air) plus 4–5 name-only; plumbers about 6 (mostly Thai SEO drain-clearing sites) plus PrapaExpert; handymen about 5; locksmiths about 6–7. Only ACES, Pattaya Home Improvement and the Na Kluea locksmith have clear 2026 activity on their own sites.
- The Thai SEO pages (ln-tradesman, thai-dd, several 24h locksmith sites) may be lead-generation networks that resell calls to whichever technician is available. Before giving one a SiamFind page, confirm it is a single business with a fixed name.
- Thai-only locksmiths and drain-clearing services are the clearest value proposition for SiamFind, because they have no English presence and expats search in English. English-capable firms (ACES, Pattaya Home Improvement, Renovate Pattaya) already have sites, so SiamFind would be an extra channel for them.
- Several East Pattaya and Thepprasit providers (Pattaya Renovators, Pattaya Electric & Air Con, the BIG KEY locksmith) suggest a cluster worth checking on Google Maps along Thepprasit and Soi Siam Country Club.

### Inferences
- Published prices are rare and mostly sit in classifieds rather than on providers' own sites. A SiamFind "starting price" field (for example call-out or hourly) would set listings apart, and a realistic opening benchmark is 300–500 THB for a call-out or first hour.
- English is mainly found in renovation and contracting firms. The 24h emergency trades (locksmith, drains) are Thai-only, which is the gap a bilingual marketplace page could fill.

### Gaps
- I found no verified current call-out fee for any locksmith in Pattaya.
- I could not confirm whether the 350 THB electrician ad (about 5 years old) and the expired Kuffki ads are still current.
- English ability for Jomtien Air and Reinhard's rests on old or third-party reports.
