Research date: 2026-11-15. Method: English and Thai web searches (ล้างแอร์ พัทยา, ติดตั้งโซล่าเซลล์ พัทยา, ติดตั้งกล้องวงจรปิด พัทยา), plus fetches of providers' own sites and directories. Limits: aseannow.com, pattayaprestigeproperties.com and pcec.club returned 403/503. ppowersolar.com and pattayakafe.com could not be resolved from this session (the network blocked them), so those providers are unverified. I could not open Facebook or Google Business pages directly. Contacts below come only from the business's own published pages or business directory listings. Forum-sourced contacts are flagged.

Excluded as instructed: BK Air Service (ช่างแอร์พัทยา.net, 095-951-7106). It appears in search results and is already on SiamFind.

## Which providers exist and are active now (2025–2026 evidence)?

### Takeaway
I found 13 aircon leads, 8 solar leads and 12 CCTV/security leads. Activity evidence is strongest for Systems Connect (blog posts from November 2026), NCA Solar, Kunini, Focus System & Design, PJ Air, ช่างแอร์พัทยา.com and ROOF21. Many aircon technicians are small, Thai-only operators whose online presence is a single keyword website. Several CCTV and alarm names seen on expat forums (Master Safety, Domotica, Secritas, IT Smart) could not be verified as currently operating.
