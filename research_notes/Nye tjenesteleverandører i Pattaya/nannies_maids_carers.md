## Which agencies/providers exist and are active now (2025–2026 evidence)?

### Takeaway
There are few agencies based in Pattaya for nannies or live-in maids. The childcare and live-in placement market is mostly served by Bangkok agencies that say they cover Pattaya: Ayasan (which has a real Pattaya office), Happy Help, ThaiKidsHome, Kiidu and HelloNanny. Pattaya has its own strong set of condo/villa cleaning companies and several English-facing nursing homes: Pattaya Nursing Home, KIN Origin Pattaya, Baan Lalisa and Homerly.

## Any licensing requirements worth noting for trust?

### Takeaway
In Thailand, placing domestic workers is a licensed activity: an employment-arrangement licence from the Department of Employment (Ministry of Labour). None of the agencies reviewed displays a licence number. Nursing and elderly care facilities fall under the Ministry of Public Health, and KIN is the only one whose site references MoPH licences (in its menu). I did not confirm the exact Health Establishment rules.

### Cited Findings
- A translated Thai law states that no one may arrange domestic employment for a jobseeker without a licence from the registrar. Licences are applied for at the provincial employment office (for Pattaya: Chonburi). Caveats: the translation is unofficial, and the consultancy source is commercial. — [Unionspace recruitment licence](https://unionspace.co.th/thailand-license-permit/recruitment-agency-license.php); [thailand.go.th](https://www.thailand.go.th/event-detail/001_01_256)
- A Ministry of Labour regulation gives domestic workers protections such as days off and minimum age. — [Tilleke & Gibbins](https://www.tilleke.com/insights/new-labor-regulation-provides-protections-domestic-workers/8/)
- Foreign (for example, migrant) domestic workers need work permits, and the procedures differ. Agencies typically handle the paperwork. — [Thailand Starter Kit](https://www.thailandstarterkit.com/moving/maid-and-nanny-in-thailand/)
- KIN's site has a "Licences (MoPH)" menu item, but the content could not be read. The other nursing homes state no licence. — [KIN Pattaya](https://kinrehab.com/kin_pattaya_ads?lang=en)

### Inferences
- **Trust badge idea:** a "Verified licence" badge on SiamFind, shown only when a provider supplies a licence number:
  - Department of Employment job-placement licence, for placement agencies
  - Ministry of Public Health / Department of Health Service Support licence, for nursing homes and elderly care centres
- Per-visit cleaning companies are not placement agencies, so the placement licence probably does not apply to them.

### Gaps
- The exact Ministry of Public Health licensing basis for elderly care centres was not confirmed. It is likely the Health Establishment Act B.E. 2559 (2016), administered by the Department of Health Service Support, but this search did not confirm it.
- The Department of Employment's online register of licensed agencies was not checked.
