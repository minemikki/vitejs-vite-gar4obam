# Building Trades in Pattaya: Lead List for SiamFind

Scope: builders and renovation contractors, painters, tilers, roofers, carpenters and custom furniture makers, interior designers, and curtain and blinds installers in the Pattaya area. Research date: 15 Nov 2026. All contacts below were taken from the provider's own website or business listing. "Not listed" means the source did not publish it. Nothing here is guessed.

Already known and not re-researched in depth: HPS Pattaya Homes, Perfect Home Pattaya, Pattaya Home Improvement, MD Pools/Loma-Tec, and Lotis Construction. A brief check found that HPS Pattaya Homes has a live kitchens and built-in page ([hpspattayahomes.com](https://hpspattayahomes.com/interiors/kitchens/)), and Pattaya Home Improvement has a live site plus an expatsthai.com directory listing ([site](https://pattayahomeimprovement.com/), [directory](https://expatsthai.com/directory/pattaya-home-improvement)). A combined search on HPS, Perfect Home, Lotis, and MD Pools returned no hits for any of them ([search returned only unrelated villa listings](https://pearlpropertythailand.com/projects)), so their status was not re-confirmed here.

## Which providers exist and are active now (2025–2026 evidence)?

### Takeaway
Builders and renovators are well covered, with about 11 leads and at least 5 showing clear 2026 activity. Curtain and blinds and interior leads are mostly Thai-language firms that serve Pattaya from Sriracha, Rayong, or Nong Prue. Painters, tilers, and roofers rarely run standalone web presences. Most of them sit inside multi-trade firms such as EPS and 1010 Tech, or in Thai LnwShop ad pages with only a phone number. Those standalone trades need Facebook and Google Maps follow-up.

## Which are foreign-friendly (English-speaking) and publish prices?

### Takeaway
Only three Pattaya building-trade leads found publish concrete prices, and all three are English-language and expat-facing: Pattaya Renovators (bathroom from 99,500 THB, kitchen from 74,900 THB), Casa Miguel (32,000–38,000 THB/m²), and Wandeegroup (12,000–39,000+ THB/m²). No painter, tiler, curtain, or roofing firm in Pattaya was found publishing prices. The only price benchmarks for those trades come from forums.

### Inferences
- Pattaya Renovators is the single strongest "foreign-friendly + published prices" lead, since it is a small Thai-registered company with condo-scale prices.
- Casa Miguel and Wandeegroup publish prices but are villa-scale and closer to developer size. They suit a "premium builders" section more than the "independent" focus.
- For curtain and blinds pages, SiamFind would likely need to ask the providers for "from" prices, because none in the Pattaya area published them online.

### Gaps
- Language support for the Thai-only curtain and interior firms is unknown, and some may have English-speaking staff without saying so.
- Whether the forum price benchmarks reflect 2026 rates is unconfirmed, because the threads are older.
