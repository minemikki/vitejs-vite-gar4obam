Contact data below is only what the business itself publishes; nothing has been guessed.

## Which providers exist and are active now (2025–2026 evidence)?

### Takeaway
I found about 6–10 usable leads per category. The best evidence of current activity comes from providers' own websites (dated footers or posts from 2025–2026) and a few Facebook pages. Tutors and music teachers are mostly small schools, plus individual tutors on marketplaces who publish no direct contacts. Pet services are mostly Thai-run Facebook-only groomers. Gardeners are mostly Thai-language local contractors. Upholstery cleaning is thin once the excluded firms are removed: LumosClean is the strongest independent lead.

### Inferences
- The upholstery category is the weakest. After the exclusions, only LumosClean is clearly active, independent and price-transparent. The other leads have either no direct contacts (321clean, PK Cleaning) or look dormant (Hello Cleany). There are probably more Thai-only operators on Facebook and LINE than web search surfaces.
- In pets, the market is dominated by small Thai groomers that only have a Facebook page. A free SiamFind shop page would be a real upgrade for them, because most have no website.
- Thai gardeners on LnwShop-style sites publish phones but often no business name. Collect the name when you first contact them.

### Gaps
- I could not read Facebook pages, so phones, LINE IDs, prices and recent-post dates for most groomers and sitters are unconfirmed.
- I could not open the Thai Pet Care site or the Mai Yen Landscape site (503 error), and no contacts showed on the Pattaya Garden Space homepage.
- I found no dedicated independent dog walker with a business presence in Jomtien, Naklua or Bang Saray.
- I found no independent English-speaking private Thai tutor with their own website in Pattaya. Individuals sit on Preply, MyPrivateTutor or italki without direct contacts.
- I found no Bang Saray-specific providers in any category.

## Which publish prices and serve expats in English?

### Takeaway
Leads with both published prices and service in English are LumosClean (sofa, mattress, carpet), Pattaya Lifestyle House Management (garden 1,000–2,500 THB/month), Easy ABC (Thai lessons by the hour) and British Learning Centre (tutoring by the hour). Individual tutors on MyPrivateTutor show hourly rates (350–1,000 THB) but publish no contacts. The pet businesses I found publish no Pattaya prices online.

### Inferences
- A SiamFind pitch to price-less providers could centre on "add a 'from X THB' price and an English description". Most Thai groomers and gardeners have neither online.
- LumosClean and Easy ABC already have structured price tables that could be copied into shop pages with little effort.

### Gaps
- I found no grooming or boarding price list published by any Pattaya pet business; these need to be asked for directly.
- Whether the Facebook-only groomers speak English is unknown.
