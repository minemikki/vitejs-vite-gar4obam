# Pattaya repair providers (IT/computer, phone/tablet, car garages, motorbike): lead list for SiamFind

Research date: 2026-11-15. Method: web search (English and Thai) plus fetching providers' own sites and directory pages. **Limits:** aseannow.com and pattaya-pages.com returned HTTP 403 to direct fetches, so what those forums say comes only from search-result snippets. Facebook pages could not be opened, so a page that exists is not proof of recent activity. Google Maps listings could not be checked. "Active 2025–2026" is confirmed only where noted. Everything else should be checked by phone or Facebook message before a page is built. Personal phone numbers posted by forum users (for example for the mechanic "Sakda") are left out on purpose.

## Which providers exist and are active now (2025–2026 evidence)?

### Takeaway
I found about 8 candidates in each category, but hard 2025–2026 activity evidence exists for only a few: Asia Design Lab (copyright 2026), the Bahtsold IT technician ad (posted 31 May 2026), Next Level Garage (recent FB posts, but "Opening Soon") and a 2025 forum mention of a Sattahip car mechanic. Most other leads come from undated provider sites (2019–2024 footers) or from older forum and directory entries.

### Inferences
- The strongest page-ready leads, with full business contacts, an own website and/or published prices, are: Authority Service, Wanchiang MB, TechMobile, Mac Pattaya, Comcare and Asia Design Lab. Motorbike shops are the weakest category, with almost no own websites and mostly Facebook-only presences.

- The Tukcom mall on South Pattaya Road holds many IT and phone stalls (Pattaya2U, WeFix, Pao Mobile). Visiting it in person would likely turn up more leads quickly.

### Gaps
- I have no independent 2025–2026 evidence that most leads are still operating. Check Google Maps "recent reviews" and the latest FB post dates.
- I found almost nothing for Naklua, East Pattaya (outside Sukhumvit), Bang Saray or Nong Prue specifically.
- No contacts found for Pattaya2U, Toon's shop, Ball, Rod, Chang Sam or Pao Mobile.

## For each: contacts, languages, published prices, pitch note

### Takeaway
Few providers publish prices. The ones that do are Authority Service (oil change from ฿1,300, A/C from ฿400), TechMobile (repair packages ฿990–3,490) and Khun Aae (about ฿300/hour). The rest are quote-only, which is a natural pitch hook for SiamFind ("publish your prices").

### Cited Findings
- Authority Service publishes a full price list. Pitch: "already price-transparent and trilingual (TH/EN/RU), and a SiamFind page would reach expats" — [Authority site](https://authoritycar.repair/)
- TechMobile publishes package prices, warranty terms and hours, but only in Thai. Pitch: "an English page for Soi Buakhao expats" — [TechMobile site](https://www.wytubeo.com/)
- Mac Pattaya offers genuine parts, a 1-year warranty and free checks, but no prices and an old 2019 site. Pitch: "a fresh, bilingual page with an Apple price menu" — [Mac Pattaya site](https://www.macpattaya.net/%E0%B8%8B%E0%B9%88%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%A1%E0%B9%84%E0%B8%AD%E0%B9%82%E0%B8%9F%E0%B8%99/index.html)
- Wanchiang MB has strong service claims but no prices. Pitch: "European-car owners in Pattaya searching in English" — [Wanchiang site](https://wanchiang-mbservice.com/en)
- Comcare and Khun Aae have no website, only directory and FB contacts. Pitch: "your own web page plus a LINE button" — [PCEC directory](https://pcec.club/COMPUTER-EQUIPMENT-AND-SERVICE)
- Market price reference (not Pattaya-specific): iPhone screens cost about 1,200 THB for old models and up to about 14,000 THB for the newest (Bangkok-based 2025 article). Glass-only examples: iPhone 13 ฿4,000, iPhone 14 Pro Max ฿5,500 — [Yuki Center 2025](https://myyukicenter.com/iphone-screen-replacement-price-2025/); [Freedom Computer Service](https://www.freedomcomputerservice.net/service/iphone/)
- Older Pattaya reports put a third-party iPhone screen at Tukcom at about 1,500 THB, done in about an hour — [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowUserReviews-g293919-d8755720-r924794181-Tukcom_Pattaya-Pattaya_Chonburi_Province.html)

### Inferences
- The language a provider supports is mostly self-declared on its own site (Authority, Asia Design Lab, rony925) or comes from expat comments (Comcare, Khun Aae, Kilin). For the Thai-only providers (TechMobile, Tonrak, WeFix, Rno, NB Benz, Chang Sam), assume Thai only until confirmed.

### Gaps
- No published prices found for any motorbike shop, or for car garages other than Authority.
- I could not read Facebook "About" sections, which may hold LINE IDs and phone numbers.

## Which are known among expats as English-speaking and honest (forum recommendations)?

The strongest expat endorsements are Comcare's Mike ("perfect English"), Wattana Systemation and Pattaya2U for IT, Toon (ex-Mityon) for motorbikes, and, for cars, a home mechanic "Sakda" (no business contact), Autoboy Na Jomtien (staff speak "quite good English") and Car Tech (Sattahip, English-owned).
